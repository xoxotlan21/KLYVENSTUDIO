1 What cookies and similar tools do

Cookies are small data files a website may place in a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and identifiers created by external scripts. They may keep a website functioning, remember choices, protect forms, measure visits, or support advertising. A tool can also collect personal information such as an IP address or an online identifier, in which case the Privacy Policy also applies.

4 Managing a choice

Where optional categories exist, a visitor may accept all, reject optional technologies, or customize the categories separately. The choice should be retained so the visitor is not asked on every page.

A persistent link in the footer allows a later change. Optional scripts should not load before the relevant choice and should respond to a later withdrawal as far as technically possible. A browser’s own settings can also remove or block stored data; this may affect site functions.
